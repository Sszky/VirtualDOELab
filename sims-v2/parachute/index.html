Fixed constants used in the model
  • Payload + parachute mass 0.12 kg.
  • Base Cd by shape: round 1.50, square 1.28, hexagon 1.40.
  • Material factor: plastic ×1.02, nylon ×1.08, paper ×0.92.
  • Gravity + quadratic air drag (½ρCdAv²), integrated every 0.005 s.
