Fixed constants used in the model
  • Paper 80 g/m² (A4) + paper clip 0.5 g.
  • Rotor acts as a disc of area π·WL²; Cd = 0.8 × (WW / 4 cm)^0.8.
  • Blades need ~0.3 s to spin up after release.
  • Gravity + quadratic air drag (½ρCdAv²), integrated with RK4 every 0.001 s.
